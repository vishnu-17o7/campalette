## Design Context

### Users
- Primary users are mobile-first creators, designers, and developers who want to sample colors from real-world scenes and quickly shape them into usable palettes.
- Usage context is on-device, often in-the-moment capture scenarios where speed and legibility matter.
- Core job to be done: capture, inspect, refine, and reuse color studies with minimal friction.

### Brand Personality
- Editorial, artisanal, and technical.
- The product voice aims to feel like a "digital atelier": premium but not sterile, expressive but still practical.
- Emotional goals inferred from current design language: confidence, creative focus, and crafted sophistication.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Warm, paper-toned surfaces with organic earthy accents.
- High-contrast serif/sans pairing (Newsreader + Manrope) to balance artistic tone and technical readability.
- Layered composition with asymmetry, rounded forms, selective gradients, and soft-glass overlays for in-camera UI.
- Current anti-goal from project docs: avoid generic template UI and avoid harsh divider-heavy layouts.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize **capture-to-feedback speed** over decorative complexity in the live camera flow.
2. Keep a **single obvious primary action** per screen; secondary actions should recede visually.
3. Preserve the **atelier voice** through typography and tone, but protect readability under real camera conditions.
4. Use visual density intentionally: **editorial richness in browsing/editing**, **operational clarity in capture**.
5. Favor explicit, teachable interactions over hidden gestures for core workflows.
