# Campalette quick start

## Capture a palette

1. Open the Camera tab.
2. Allow camera access when you want to capture a scene. You can choose **Import a photo instead** without granting camera access.
3. Point the camera at the colors you want and tap the shutter.
4. Tap the captured image to sample another color. Hold a point to explore related hues.
5. Save the result or open the palette editor.

Campalette processes camera frames and selected photos on the device.

## Refine and save

Open **Edit palette** after a capture, or select a study from **Library**.

- Rename the palette.
- Choose an analogous, complementary, or tonal harmony.
- Tap a swatch to use it as the harmony seed.
- Hold and drag a swatch to reorder it.
- Open a color to inspect values, preview color-vision differences, or remove it.
- Tap **Save** to keep the palette in the local library.

## Share and export

Open the share sheet from the editor to:

- Share a palette image
- Copy all HEX values
- Copy CSS variables
- Copy Swift `UIColor` code
- Copy Android color resources
- Copy Figma JSON

Android sends shared content only to the app you choose.

## Appearance and feedback

Open **Settings** to choose system, light, or dark appearance, control palette accent colors, reduce motion, and disable haptics. The privacy policy and installed version appear in the About group.
