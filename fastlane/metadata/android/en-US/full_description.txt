Campalette turns the colors around you into palettes you can refine, save, and reuse.

Point the camera at a scene or choose a photo, then extract a balanced color study in seconds. Tap a captured image to sample an exact color, explore related harmonies, and shape the result in a focused palette editor.

Built for designers, artists, developers, and photographers:

- Capture palettes from the live camera
- Import a specific image with Android's privacy-friendly photo picker
- Sample individual colors from captured scenes
- Build analogous, complementary, and tonal harmonies
- Reorder, remove, and inspect palette colors
- Preview colors for common color-vision differences
- Save palettes locally for later
- Share a clean palette image
- Copy HEX values or export CSS, Swift, Android XML, and Figma JSON
- Choose light, dark, or system appearance
- Reduce motion or disable haptic feedback

Campalette is offline-first. Camera frames and selected photos are processed on your device. The app contains no accounts, ads, or analytics.
