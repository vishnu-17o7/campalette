## What the app does

Campalette captures colors from the camera or from a photo you pick, then lets you refine, save, and export palettes on your device.

Palettes, names, and settings you create on your device belong to you. Campalette does not claim ownership of those files.

## On-device processing

Camera frames and photos you select are processed on your device. Campalette does not operate a server and does not upload that media to the developer. If you use Android’s share sheet, the app or service you choose receives only the palette image or text you shared.
