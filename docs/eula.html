What the app does

Campalette captures colors from the camera or from a photo you pick, then lets you refine, save, and export palettes on your device. Palettes, names, and settings you create belong to you.

On-device processing

Camera frames and photos you select are processed on your device. Campalette does not operate a server and does not upload that media to the developer. If you use Android’s share sheet, the destination you choose receives only what you shared.
