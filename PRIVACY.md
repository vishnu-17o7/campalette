# Campalette Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 17, 2026  
Last updated: September 5, 2026

Campalette is an offline-first color capture and palette design app. This policy explains what the app accesses and how that information is handled.

## Data Campalette accesses

- **Camera:** When you grant camera permission, Campalette uses the live camera preview and captured frames to extract colors. Camera images are processed on your device and are not uploaded by Campalette.
- **Photos you select:** The system photo picker lets you choose a specific image for palette extraction. Campalette does not request access to your full photo library.
- **Palette data and settings:** Saved palettes, capture history, and preferences are stored locally on your device. If Android backup or device-to-device transfer is enabled on your device, this small app-state file may be included in a backup controlled by your platform account.

Campalette does not read your advertising ID, contacts, location, SMS, call log, or full media library.

## Data collection and sharing

Campalette does not operate a server, create user accounts, include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or social SDKs, or transmit personal data to the developer.

When you explicitly use Android's share sheet, the palette image or text you chose is sent to the app or service you select. That recipient handles the shared content under its own privacy policy. Campalette treats that transfer as user-initiated.

## Permissions

- `CAMERA` is used only for live color sampling and capture.
- `VIBRATE` is used for optional tactile feedback.

You can revoke camera access or disable haptics at any time in Android or Campalette settings.

## Data deletion

Uninstalling Campalette removes its local app data. If Android backup is enabled, you can manage or delete device backups through your Google account settings.

## Children's privacy

Campalette is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes and contact

Material changes will be published in this file with a revised last-updated date. Questions can be opened through the public [Campalette issue tracker](https://github.com/vishnu-17o7/campalette/issues).
